## About Me

I work with BFSI data at **Choice TechLab, Mumbai**, turning Equity, Insurance, Wealth, Banking and Credit Card data into dashboards and reports that business teams use every day.

My approach is simple: find the real question, write the SQL, build the dashboard, then automate the report so nobody has to send it by hand again.

I started as a Data Analytics Intern and moved to a full-time role within six months. I came to Mumbai from Solapur, and I'm building my career step by step: **Data Analyst → Data Engineer**.

**Open to:** Data Analyst · BI Analyst · MIS / Reporting Analyst · Junior Data Engineer (Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Pune, Remote)
