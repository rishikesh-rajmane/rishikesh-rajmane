## About

I work with BFSI data at **Choice TechLab, Mumbai**. My job is to turn Equity, Insurance, Wealth, Banking and Credit Card data into reports that business heads actually use, and then automate those reports so nobody has to send them by hand.

I started as a Data Analytics Intern and moved to a full-time role within six months. I'm building toward **Data Engineering** with ETL pipelines and AWS.

**Open to:** Data Analyst · BI Analyst · MIS / Reporting Analyst · Junior Data Engineer, in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Pune or remote.
